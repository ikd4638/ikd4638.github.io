Teaching Philosophy

I believe that effective teaching goes beyond transmitting knowledge—it involves inspiring curiosity, fostering critical thinking, and building confidence. In my courses, I emphasize:

  • Conceptual clarity - Building strong foundations before moving to complex topics
  • Hands-on learning - Incorporating computational exercises and data analysis projects
  • Research-integrated teaching - Connecting course material to current research frontiers
  • Inclusive environment - Encouraging questions and diverse perspectives

I welcome students from all backgrounds who are eager to learn about the fascinating physics of black holes, accretion flows, and high-energy astrophysical phenomena.

Students interested in working with me on black hole physics, GRMHD simulations, or related topics are welcome to contact me via email.
